LDI Replication

Stata MATLAB R Python
Context Undergraduate Research
Organization American Economic Association
Location Ithaca, NY

Overview

The LDI Replication project is part of the American Economic Association's initiative to improve research transparency and methodological rigor across empirical studies. This work involves evaluating the computational and statistical replicability of manuscripts and datasets from research labs nationwide, ensuring that reported results are internally consistent and reproducible.

In this role, I reconstruct data pipelines using various statistical and programming languages to verify the integrity of research findings. I work across complex datasets and codebases to identify inconsistencies between documented methodology, actual implementation, and reported results. My contributions help researchers strengthen their work and contribute to broader improvements in research transparency and reproducibility standards.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assess computational and statistical replicability of manuscripts and datasets from research labs nationwide
  • Reconstruct data pipelines to verify that reported results are internally consistent and reproducible
  • Identify inconsistencies between code, data, and written methodology, communicating findings clearly to researchers
  • Critically evaluate research design, identification strategies, statistical assumptions, and robustness checks
  • Train incoming lab technicians on replication methodologies and best practices

Technologies & Tools Used

  • Stata: Statistical analysis and data manipulation
  • MATLAB: Computational and numerical analysis
  • R: Statistical computing and graphics
  • Python: Data processing and reproducibility verification
